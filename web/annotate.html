Guidelines

Annotate independently. Do not open the model's predictions or your teammate's file before you finish.
  • Tag the smallest span that names the concept: "breast cancer", not "had breast cancer".
  • Tag mentions in questions too, with assertion UNKNOWN ("Do you have fever?").
  • PRESENT = patient affirms · ABSENT = patient denies · POSSIBLE = unsure · UNKNOWN = question / not established.
  • Demographic values (age, phone, income...) use assertion PRESENT.
  • Click a highlight to delete it. Download often; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
