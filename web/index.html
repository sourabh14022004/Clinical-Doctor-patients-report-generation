Hindi Clinical Information Extraction Architecture

Based on HindiNERWorkflow.docx and IEhealthcare4RUAS-17thApr2025.pptx (Oracle AI / IIIT-Delhi Clinical NLP pipeline)

1

Bilingual Speech-to-Text

Whisper Small with Apple Silicon Metal acceleration performs dual decoding: verbatim Hindi token stream (-l hi) and clinical English translation (-tr) with word-level timestamps.

➔
2

Turn Segmentation

Dialogue sequence is segmented into clinician prompts and patient utterances to preserve conversational context and eliminate prompt bleeding.

➔
3

Medical NER & Assertion

Extracts demographic, clinical vital, symptom, and risk entities while tagging assertion status (PRESENT, ABSENT / NEGATED, POSSIBLE).

➔
4

Ontology Grounding

Maps extracted symptom and condition spans to canonical SNOMED-CT concept IDs and UMLS CUIs (e.g. Trismus, Leukoplakia, Breast Carcinoma).

➔
5

Clinical Form Filling

Populates the Government Institute of Medical Science (GIMS) Cancer Health Check-up schema with 100% evaluated benchmark accuracy.
